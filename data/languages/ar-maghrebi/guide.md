# Maghrebi Arabic (الدارجة المغاربية)

Shared core of Maghrebi Arabic (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya). Country guides override these, and the countries differ a lot in everyday words.

## Pronunciation

- Short vowels drop and consonants cluster: "كتب" said ktəb, "نكتب" nəktəb. Writing follows speech, not MSA vowel patterns.
- ق is q in many cities and g in rural/Bedouin speech; varies by region and word.
- Arabizi (Latin letters) is very common: 3 (ع), 7 (ح), 9 (ق), and French-style spellings: ch (ش), ou (و), é. Tunisians and Libyans often use 5 (خ); Moroccans and Algerians more often write kh.
- French words and whole French phrases are normal, especially in cities: ميرسي، بورطابل (phone), "ça va", "normal". Code-switching is not a mistake.

## Grammar & markers

- First person singular uses نـ: "نكتب" (I write); plural adds ـو: "نكتبو" (we write), "كتبو" (they wrote). Not "أكتب".
- Negation wraps the verb: ما...ش ("ما نعرفش", I don't know; "ما فهمتش"). Nominal negation differs by country.
- Shared question words: علاش (why), كيفاش (how), شكون (who), وقتاش (when). "What" and "where" vary (آش / شنو / واش / شنوّة; فين / وين).
- Possessive "of" is a strong country marker: ديال (Morocco), تاع (Algeria), متاع (Tunisia, Libya).
- Future, continuous, "want", "now" and "a lot" all differ by country; see the country guide.

## Common AI mistakes

- Mashriqi words: Egyptian (عايز، دلوقتي، إزيّك، أوي), Levantine (بدّي، هلّق، كتير، شو), Gulf (أبي، وايد، الحين).
- MSA verb forms: "أكتب"، "لا أعرف"، سوف، لماذا instead of "نكتب"، "ما نعرفش".
- Blending the countries: ديال with تاع، بزاف with برشا، دابا with توّا. Stay in one country's forms.
- Avoiding French when the user mixes it in, or forcing French when they write pure Arabic.
- Treating "Darija" as only Moroccan.

## Natural usage

- Match the user's script exactly: Arabic script, Arabizi, or a French mix. Many people switch mid-sentence.
- Keep it plain and friendly; do not stack slang or proverbs.
- If the country is unclear, avoid the country-marked words above and use shared forms (ما...ش، علاش، كيفاش).
