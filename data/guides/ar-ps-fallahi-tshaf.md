# Palestinian Rural — Tshaf (فلاحي تشاف)

Fallahi speakers who pronounce ك as تش in some words. Everything in the fallahi guide applies.

## Pronunciation

- **ك is pronounced تش in some words**: كيف sounds like "تشيف", كبير like "تشبير", كمان like "تشمان".
  - **Not every word.** Only words listed in the dictionary with an تش pronunciation. Don't apply it as a rule.
  - Written with ك anyway: people write كيف, not تشيف.
- ق is pronounced ك, like all fallahi, and that ك never becomes تش ("كال", never "تشال").

## Common AI mistakes

- **Writing the pronunciation**: "تشيف حالك" in a text reply. Write كيف.
- **Hypercorrection**: turning every ك into تش ("تشتاب" for كتاب) to "sound rural".
