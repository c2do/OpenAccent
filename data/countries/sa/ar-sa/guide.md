# Saudi Arabic (سعودي)

Saudi dialects differ a lot. Najdi (Riyadh, central) and Hijazi (Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah) are the main ones; the Eastern Province is close to the shared Gulf guide.

## Pronunciation

- ج stays ج in Najd and Hijaz (unlike Kuwait/Bahrain/Qatar/UAE). Do not write "ريّال" for man; use رجّال.
- ق → g in both. ك → ts/ch only in traditional or rural Najdi ("عليتس"); urban young Najdi and Hijazi keep ك.
- Hijazi often says ث → ت/س and ذ → د/ز ("كدا" for كذا, "هادا" for هذا), and people write it that way. Najdi keeps ث and ذ.

## Grammar & markers

- Negation: ما with verbs everywhere. With nouns: مو (both); Najdi also مهوب / مهب and ماني ("ماني فاضي").
- Future: Najdi بـ ("بروح"); Hijazi حـ or راح ("حروح"، "راح أروح").
- Continuous: قاعد / جالس + verb ("قاعد أشتغل"); Hijazi also uses بـ for ongoing/habitual ("بيشتغل").
- Want: أبي (more Najdi), أبغى / أبغا (Hijazi, and common nationwide).
- What: وش (Najdi), إيش (Hijazi). How: وشلون / كيف (Najdi), كيف (Hijazi). Why: ليش / ليه.
- Demonstratives: Najdi هذا، هذي، ذا، ذي، هذولا; Hijazi هادا، هادي، دا، دي، هادول.
- Now: الحين (Najdi), دحين (Hijazi). Very: مرّة is common across Saudi ("مرّة حلو").

## Common AI mistakes

- Using Kuwaiti/Emirati forms: شنو، شو، مب، ج → ي spellings, and overusing وايد.
- Egyptian creeping into Hijazi because they sound close: عايز، دلوقتي، أوي، إزيّك، خالص. Hijazi uses أبغى، دحين، مرّة.
- Mixing Najdi and Hijazi in one reply (وش with دحين، هادا with مهوب). Pick one.
- MSA stiffness (لماذا، سوف) or over-formal honorifics in casual chat.

## Natural usage

- Default to neutral urban Saudi (أبغى / أبي، وش or إيش matching the user, مرّة، الحين) unless the user shows Hijazi or Najdi markers.
- Friendly phrases like "حيّاك"، "يعطيك العافية" are natural; don't repeat them every message.
- Mirror the user's spelling, including Arabizi if they use it.
