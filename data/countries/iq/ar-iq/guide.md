# Iraqi Arabic (عراقي)

Iraqi Arabic, with Baghdadi as the default. Southern and northern (Mosul) speech differ; follow the user if they show a regional form. Iraqi is not Levantine or Gulf.

## Pronunciation

- ق is often /g/, written گ or ق ("گال" / "قال" = said, "گلب" = heart). Some words keep /q/ ("قرآن").
- ك is often "ch", written چ or ك ("چان" = was, "چبير" = big), and always in the feminine "you" suffix: "شلونچ"، "بيتچ".
- ث, ذ, ظ are kept, not turned into ت/د/ز ("ثلاثة"، "هذا").
- In chat, people use گ and چ freely, or fall back to ق/ك/ج. Mirror whichever the user uses.

## Grammar & markers

- I: آني. We: إحنا. You pl.: إنتو. They: همّه.
- Negation: ما for verbs ("ما أدري"، "ما أعرف"); مو for nouns and adjectives ("مو زين"، "مو هيچ").
- There is / there isn't: أكو / ماكو ("أكو وقت؟"، "ماكو مشكلة").
- Future: راح or رح ("راح أروح").
- Present: plain verb with no بـ prefix ("أروح"، "تعرف؟"). Continuous: دـ / دا ("دأكتب"، "دا يشتغل").
- Want: أريد ("أريد أروح"، "شتريد؟").
- Demonstratives: هذا (this, m.), هاي (this, f.), هذوله (these); ذاك / ذيچ (that).
- Question words: شنو (what), منو (who), وين (where), ليش (why), شوكت (when), شلون (how), شگد / شكد (how much).
- Common: هسّه (now), هواية (a lot), كلّش (very), زين (good), خوش (good, before a noun: "خوش فكرة"), شكو ماكو؟ (what's up), عيني (dear).

## Common AI mistakes

- Levantine words: بدّي (use أريد), شو (use شنو), كتير (use هواية), هلّق (use هسّه), and the بـ prefix ("بحكي" is not Iraqi).
- Gulf words: وايد (use هواية), أبي / أبغى (use أريد), الحين (use هسّه). Gulf and Iraqi share some forms (شلون, چ), which makes this leak easy.
- Egyptian words: دلوقتي, إزيّك, أوي, عايز.
- MSA: سوف (use راح), لماذا (use ليش), ماذا (use شنو), ليس (use مو), لا يوجد (use ماكو).
- Using "Bedouin" or southern forms for a Baghdadi user, or overusing endearments like "عيني" and "يمعود".

## Natural usage

- Tone is warm and direct. Mirror the user's length and register. Spelling varies: هسه/هسّه, شكد/شگد, چ/ك. Match the user rather than normalising to MSA.
