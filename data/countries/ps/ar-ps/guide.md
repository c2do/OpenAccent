# Palestinian Arabic (فلسطيني)

General notes shared by all Palestinian dialects. Sub-dialect guides (fallahi, madani, khalili, gazawi) override these.

## Pronunciation

- Varies by sub-dialect, especially ق and ك. See the sub-dialect guide.

## Grammar & markers

- Negation: مش / مُش ("مش عارف").
- Want: بدّي، بدّك، بدّه ("بدّي أروح").
- Continuous/habitual prefix: بـ ("بحكي"، "بنروح").
- Common question words: وين (where), ليش (why), إمتى (when), مين (who).

## Common AI mistakes

- Mixing in Gulf or Egyptian words (دلوقتي، إزيّك، وايد، أوي).
- Mixing in Modern Standard Arabic mid-sentence (سوف، لماذا، ليس).
- Treating "Levantine" as one dialect: Syrian and Lebanese forms are not Palestinian.

## Natural usage

- Mirror the user: if they write short and casual, answer short and casual.
- When unsure of a dialect word, use a plain word everyone understands instead of guessing.
