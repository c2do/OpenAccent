# Code of Conduct

OpenAccent collects how people really talk, from cities, villages and countries that don't always get along. Everyone is welcome here, and every dialect is treated with respect.

## Expected

- Treat every dialect as a full way of speaking. None is "wrong", "broken" or "less Arabic" (or less of any language).
- Assume good faith. People describe the speech of their own family and town; two correct answers can differ by a few kilometres.
- Disagree about words with sources and examples, not with people.

## Not accepted

- Mocking a dialect, accent, region, nationality, religion or community.
- Harassment, slurs aimed at people, or personal attacks. (Slurs can be *documented* as dictionary entries, labelled `sensitive`; they are never used against anyone here.)
- Political arguments that have nothing to do with the words in front of us.
- Publishing anyone's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and contributions, and block people who break these rules. Report problems by opening an issue marked "conduct", or by contacting the maintainer listed on the repository profile. Reports are handled privately.
